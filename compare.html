The philosophical difference

本質上的差別

Every launchd GUI before Clocksmith is an editor: it helps you write the configuration file, then its job is done. Clocksmith is a monitor first: its core question is "did last night's job actually run, and did it succeed?" It reconstructs run history from the macOS system log — start, end, duration, exit code — which, as of our July 2026 survey, no other launchd GUI does. Editing (daily / weekly / every-N-minutes schedules, create, enable, disable, run now) is deliberately kept simple.

Clocksmith 之前的所有 launchd 圖形工具本質上都是編輯器：幫你把設定檔寫好，任務就結束了。Clocksmith 則以監控為先：它回答的核心問題是「昨晚的排程真的跑了嗎？成功了嗎？」它從 macOS 系統日誌重建執行歷史——開始、結束、歷時、結束代碼——以 2026 年 7 月的調查來說，這是市場上獨一份。編輯功能（每天／每週／每 N 分鐘、建立、啟用停用、立即執行）則刻意保持簡單。

Who should buy what — honestly

誰該買哪個——老實說

Buy LaunchControl if…

買 LaunchControl，如果你…

…you're a developer or sysadmin who needs the full plist vocabulary: WatchPaths, KeepAlive, socket activation, resource limits, system-level daemons. It's the most capable tool in this space, very actively maintained, and worth its price for professionals. Clocksmith doesn't try to compete there.

…你是工程師或系統管理員，需要完整的 plist 語彙：WatchPaths、KeepAlive、socket 啟動、資源限制、系統層級 daemon。它是這個領域功能最強、更新最勤的工具，對專業人士物有所值。Clocksmith 沒打算在這塊跟它競爭。

Use Lingon if…

用 Lingon，如果你…

…you mainly want a guided, friendly way to create a job once and forget it, and the free Mac App Store version covers your needs. Solid, long-maintained app.

…你主要想要一個友善的精靈，建立排程之後就不管了，而且免費的 Mac App Store 版就夠用。老牌、穩定的 app。

Use the free open-source tools if…

用免費開源工具，如果你…

…you're comfortable with unsigned builds and just want a quick list view. Check maintenance status first — several have known-broken features.

…你不介意未簽名的程式，只想要個簡單的清單畫面。用之前先確認維護狀態——有幾個的功能已知是壞的。

Buy Clocksmith if…

買 Clocksmith，如果你…

…you have jobs that matter — backups, sync scripts, AI-generated automations — and what keeps you up at night is "did it silently fail?" Run history with exit codes, red-flagged error lines, a failure banner, and one-click AI diagnosis answer that in seconds. It's also the cheapest paid option, and the only one with the monitoring angle.

…你有不能失敗的排程——備份、同步腳本、AI 幫你設的自動化——而你最怕的是「它悄悄失敗了我卻不知道」。執行歷史＋結束代碼、錯誤行標紅、失敗橫幅、一鍵 AI 診斷，幾秒鐘就給你答案。它同時是付費選項裡最便宜的，也是唯一主打監控的。
